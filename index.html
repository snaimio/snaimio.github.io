About

I build native apps on both major mobile platforms rather than specializing in just one — SwiftUI on iOS, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android. That dual footing shows up in how I work: I care about platform conventions (Apple's HIG, Material Design) as much as the code underneath them.

Outside of coursework, I use AI tools deliberately across the dev workflow — for scaffolding, debugging, and code review — while keeping the judgment calls on architecture and quality my own.
